MATH 212 Linear Algebra and Differential Equations 4 Credits
Systems of linear equations; Gaussian elimination. Matrices and matrix operations. Vector spaces, subspaces, linear independence, basis, dimension. Linear transformations, matrix representations, change of bases. Determinants. Eigenvalues, eigenvectors. First-order differential equations and solution methods. Higher-order linear differential equations and solution methods.
