EE 415 Digital Speech and Audio Processing 3 Credits
Linear Predictive modelling of Speech. Pitch Estimation Speech Modelling. Speech Coding using Linear Predictive methods: LPC-10, CELP, MELP algorithms. Speech recognition, and synthesis. Audio processing and coding, MPEG standard.
