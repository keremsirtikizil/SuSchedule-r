EE 419 Signal Processing Design and Implementation 3 Credits
Development of real-time digital signal processing (DSP) systems using a DSP microprocessor; several structured laboratory exercises, such as sampling and real-time time discrete-time filtering, median filtering, FFT implementation. DSP projects.
