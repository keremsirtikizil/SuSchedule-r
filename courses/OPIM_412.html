OPIM 412 Strategic Procurement 3 Credits
The goal of this course is to examine the effects of operational, tactical and strategic purchasing processes and supplier relationship management on organizational performance. Topics covered include purchasing processes, supplier selection and evaluation, supplier relationship management, global sourcing and risk management, supplier involvement in new product development, and sustainable purchasing.
