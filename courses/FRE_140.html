FRE 140 Intermediate French II 3 Credits
At this level, students consolidate their language knowledge are provided with opportunities to practice their language skills. They work with a variety of text types, including newspaper and magazine articles. The content of the learning materials focuses on many everyday topics that stimulate lively discussion, encouraging students to express their opinions freely.
