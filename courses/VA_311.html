VA 311 Art and Design Practices in the City 3 Credits
The goal of this course is to introduce the students to the different kinds, sizes, modes and formats of art, design and cultural institutions / venues and circles in Istanbul. The team of students and the course professor / TA visit different venues and meet scholars, professionals, managers, directors and various individuals who work in pertinent fields. Through the excursions to be made physically in the city and introductions, the students gain a deeper understanding of the art, design, and culture scene in Istanbul and its significance in the global context. They will also have the opportunity to engage in critical artistic, cultural, managerial, social, economic discussions and analyses of the institutions and individuals they encounter.
