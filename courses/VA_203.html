VA 203 Language of Drawing I 4 Credits
This introductory studio course presents drawing as a fundamental practice of observation, expression, and spatial thinking. Open to students from all disciplines, it explores drawing beyond technical skill, emphasizing its role as a tool for inquiry and communication across fields. Through diverse approaches and critical discussions, participants engage with mark-making, composition, and representation, developing a personal visual language applicable to their own practices.
