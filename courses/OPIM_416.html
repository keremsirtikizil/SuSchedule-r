OPIM 416 Quality Improvement with Six Sigma 3 Credits
Six Sigma is a disciplined and data-driven methodology for eliminating defects and improving the quality of processes and products, from manufacturing to transactional and from tangible goods to services. This course provides a thorough understanding of the Six Sigma methodology and its applications throughout the organization. Each of the five phases of DMAIC roadmap namely, Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve & Control along with their appropriate tools and techniques are instructed in depth and along with real case examples. At the end of this course, the students will have enough experience to become a team member in a Six Sigma DMAIC project at the Green Belt level and be able to run a small sized project.
