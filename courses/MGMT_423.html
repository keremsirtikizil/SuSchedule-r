MGMT 423 Entrepreneurial Finance 3 Credits
The course aims to equip students with the analytical and managerial capabilities necessary to design financially viable entrepreneurial business models, evaluate financing needs, and develop and apply appropriate funding strategies. The course is designed for both aspiring entrepreneurs and students seeking careers within the entrepreneurial ecosystem, including incubators, accelerators, venture capital firms, and related initiatives.
