OPIM 390 Introduction to Business Analytics 3 Credits
As an introductory course to this Minor Honors Program, the course will cover topics on the conceptual framework of business analytics, various sectoral application areas and a general introduction to analytical methods used. The course will also cover success stories from different sectors where business analytics is applied, and big data analytics in general, including its application areas, as a new and emerging area of interest.
