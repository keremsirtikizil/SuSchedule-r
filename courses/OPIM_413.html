OPIM 413 AI in Business 3 Credits
AI in Business explores how artificial intelligence (AI) is used to transform modern organizations, from strategy and operations to marketing and decision-making. This course balances managerial insights with hands-on experience in AI tools and applications. Students will examine core concepts such as machine learning, generative AI, and agentic systems, while engaging with real-world case studies across industries. Through interactive projects, they will gain practical skills in leveraging AI for business innovation, as well as a critical understanding of its ethical, strategic, and organizational implications.
