VA 267 Integrated Industrial Design & Innovation Studio 3 Credits
This course integrates industrial design thinking, materials science, innovation, and entrepreneurial strategy within a single studio framework. Students explore how technological feasibility, sustainable production, and market awareness shape design outcomes. Through iterative prototyping and critical reflection, participants develop products and systems that balance aesthetic, functional, and ethical values.
