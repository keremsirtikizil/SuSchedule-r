SPA 120 Basic Spanish II 3 Credits
This course involves a quick review of the topics covered at the Basic level. The students at this level learn to exchange information about the past, including biographical information and stories. They learn to use everyday expressions and to ask for help, advice and guidance and accept/decline offers. Students also develop discussion skills and learn to present arguments. Reading work continues with the books appropriate to the students' level.
